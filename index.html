🌴 Jungle Pop & Move

Learn letter sounds by moving!

  1. Cast or mirror this screen to your TV (landscape works best).
  2. Prop the phone up so the front camera can see your child standing about 2 metres back.
  3. Turn the volume up, then tap the button below.

Privacy: the camera picture stays on this device. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. (The body-tracking tool and its model are downloaded when you tap start, so you need to be online.)
